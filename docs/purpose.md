# Purpose and who it is for

This repo helps a reviewer answer one question: should this AI idea go
further, and what is missing before it may? It is for risk reviewers,
finance managers, and engineers who must say no with reasons, not just
with a feeling.

It is not a model, not a vendor product, and not a live system. The
firm in every example, Fernwick Bank, is invented. All rows, prices,
and scores are synthetic, chosen to show the mechanics clearly.

The toolkit covers five jobs:

1. Score an idea on danger, clarity of reasoning, and record-keeping,
   with a rule that blocks deployment when any pillar is weak.
2. Price model calls and check the result against a budget.
3. Review security across secrecy, correctness, and uptime.
4. Weigh building a capability against buying one, with weights shown.
5. Grade a claim by the evidence behind it, so a sales claim and a
   live system are never confused.
