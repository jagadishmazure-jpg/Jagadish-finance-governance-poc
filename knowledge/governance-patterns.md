# Knowledge — governance patterns

- One weak pillar blocks. Averages hide failures; minimums reveal them.
- Prices need dates. A price without a date is a guess wearing a number.
- Checklists need owners. An unchecked item with no owner stays unchecked.
- Evidence needs a list first. Grading without a published list is opinion.
- Approvals need a name, a scope, and an expiry. Anything less is a
  rubber stamp.
- Logs need redaction before they are written, not after they leak.
- Caps need errors, not silent pages. A caller who gets a partial page
  without warning will act on partial data.
