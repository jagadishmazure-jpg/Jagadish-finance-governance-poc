# Knowledge — evidence grades in detail

## Live grade

Needs: records of real use, a log of incidents, a sample audit
trail, and a performance report. If any one is missing, the idea is
not live-graded, whatever its supporters say.

## Trial grade

Needs: a written trial scope, trial results, and a list of known
limits. A trial without stated limits is not a trial grade; it is an
idea with a demo.

## Idea stage

Needs: a concept description. Ideas are valuable and must be named
as ideas, so nobody budgets, staffs, or promises them as live tools.

## Downgrade rule

Grading always walks down from the strongest grade: check the live
set first, then the trial set, then the idea set. The first fully
satisfied set is the awarded grade. The gap between claimed and
awarded grade is the review finding.
